SeatServe v2.0.3 - Checkout & Payments

Adds Administration > Settings > Checkout & payments with workspace-specific controls for:
- Delivery fee
- Tax rate
- Estimated card fee percentage
- Estimated fixed card fee
- Enable exact cash at delivery
- Enable credit card at delivery

Customer checkout now:
- Shows exact cash total
- Shows estimated card fee
- Shows credit card total
- Lets the customer choose cash or card at delivery
- Saves payment method and both totals with the order

Install:
1. Extract into the SeatServe folder, replacing matching files.
2. In app, run npm install.
3. Run npm run dev.
4. Open Administration > Settings.
